# Lead Magnet Strategy & Implementation Plan

This document outlines the proposed lead magnets identified in the SEO keyword strategy. These will be implemented in a subsequent session.

## 1. Robotic Coffee ROI Calculator
**Goal:** Capture high-intent leads in the Decision stage (Group 1 & 3).

### Content & Structure
- **Target Audience:** Hotel Managers, Corporate Facility Managers.
- **Inputs:**
  - Current daily coffee volume.
  - Current cost of labor (hourly rate + benefits).
  - Number of shifts/baristas.
  - Planned deployment duration.
- **Outputs:**
  - Estimated monthly savings.
  - Break-even point (months).
  - Total 3-year projected ROI.
- **Location:** Dedicated landing page (`/solutions/roi-calculator/`) and embedded in Solution pages (Compact & Craft).

---

## 2. Technical Specification Guide for Venue Integration
**Goal:** Capture leads for large-scale deployments and concession RFP requirements (Group 2).

### Content & Structure
- **Target Audience:** Concession Holders, Airport Terminal Managers, Architects.
- **Key Sections:**
  - Electrical requirements (Voltage, Peak Load).
  - Water & Drainage connectivity specs.
  - Floor loading and footprint diagrams.
  - Telemetry and Network requirements.
  - Safety & Certification compliance (CE, FCC, ISO).
- **Format:** High-quality PDF download.
- **Location:** Featured on High-Volume Solution page and Iris product pages.

---

## 3. Deployment & Integration Checklist
**Goal:** Support the "Awareness" to "Consideration" transition.

### Content & Structure
- **Sections:**
  - Site survey checklist.
  - Consumable supply chain setup.
  - Staff training overview.
  - Software & Fleet Management app setup.
- **Location:** Blog post footer and "Resources" section.
