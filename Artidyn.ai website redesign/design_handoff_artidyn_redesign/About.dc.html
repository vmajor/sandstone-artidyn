About Artidyn

We test the machines so you don't have to.

Artidyn Robotics is a two-person engineering and sourcing firm with a simple position: the beverage-robotics market is full of unverified claims, and buyers deserve tested numbers, transparent pricing, and a partner who stays after installation.

We vet

We source machines through a supply chain we know first-hand, and reject far more than we certify.

Throughput, serve time, and reliability figures on this site come from our own certification testing, not brochures.

We stay

Installation, training, calibration, and support after launch. A machine that stops earning is our problem too.
